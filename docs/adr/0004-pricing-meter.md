# Pricing meter: verdict-bearing verified runs, never seats

The PAYG meter is the **verdict-bearing verified run** — one per-PR invocation that executes the manifest's checks and produces an evidence bundle with a verdict, regardless of check count, duration, or substrate — plus a team subscription for retention, RBAC, and the hosted hub. Compute (sandbox/LLM) is passed through near cost as a separate, visible line item, never blended into the meter, because that layer is commoditized (~$0.05/vCPU-hr at E2B/Daytona). Seat pricing is explicitly rejected: the market is moving off seats — Copilot to usage credits, Bugbot dropped $40/seat for per-run, Devin sells ACUs with unlimited seats (`docs/research/business-model.md` §1.4) — and a seat meter would pay us less the more autonomous our customers' agents become, the opposite of our thesis.
